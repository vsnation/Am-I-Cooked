# Venue walk — user test form (~20 people)

App: **Am I Cooked** — paste a wallet, get a cooked-verdict autopsy; recovery actions
(revoke) are gated by World Selfie Check and signed in World App.

## Walker script (say this, ~15s)

> "90-second test of a hackathon app — it roasts a crypto wallet. No names, no contact
> info, nothing stored about you — I only tick anonymous boxes. Want to try?"

Rules: testers are numbered T01–T20, never named. Hand them the phone with the app open
(or let them scan the QR to the live URL). Don't explain the verdict — watch whether it
explains itself. Fill one row per tester, quotes verbatim.

## Privacy note (read on request)

We collect zero personal data: no names, no photos, no wallet addresses of testers.
The form records only anonymous tester numbers and yes/no reactions. Selfie Check runs
entirely inside World App — our app receives only a boolean verification result and
persists nothing about the tester.
