The Paperwork: an autopsy report you could file in a drawer

The morgue half of the brand taken literally. Cream stock, typewriter fields, a rubber stamp for the verdict, no glow anywhere. Heat exists only as printed ink on a thermal scale, so the doneness reads as a measurement rather than a mood. All four screens below.

Why this one could win

Palette
Paper #F2EBDD, carbon #171310, stamp blood #A8291E, pencil blue-grey #4E5A63, thermal ochre #C8781F, healed ink #2F6B48.
Type
A condensed grotesque does the shouting (score, stamps, headers), an old-style serif carries the roast and the thesis, and a typewriter face holds every field label and machine value — three voices, one document.
Layout
A ruled form on a baseline grid: label gutter left, values right, punch holes down the spine. The verdict is a stamp struck across the form, not a dashboard widget.
Risk
Light and quiet in a feed of dark crypto apps — that contrast is the whole bet. It also drops every emoji, which costs some of v7's warmth and speed of read.
Trade-off
Strongest for the share card and the trust story; weakest for the demo's dopamine — there is no needle to watch climb.
