The Evidence Log: treat the wallet like a crime scene, not a meme

No mascot, no gauge, no emoji. Monospace throughout, hairline rules, timestamps down the left edge, and colour used only where it means something: red is a live threat, amber is a finding, green is closed. The jokes still land — they just arrive as evidence.

Why this one could win

Palette
Void #0A0C0D, bone #DCDED9, dim #7D8489, critical #E2503C, warn #D99A2B, cleared #57A07A. Semantic only — there is no decorative accent anywhere.
Type
One monospace family at six sizes, doing all the work — the hierarchy comes from size, tracking and rules rather than from contrasting faces. Every figure is tabular so columns of money line up down the screen.
Layout
Fixed top bar, scrolling body, status bar pinned at the bottom: a tool, not a page. Findings carry a severity stripe and a right-aligned amount; the operations log proves the 40-second claim with timestamps instead of an animation.
Risk
The deliberate one: it throws away the mascot, the dial and every emoji — the assets that make v7 charming — and bets that credibility spreads further than cuteness.
Trade-off
Strongest for the judges' trust questions and for the reusable-skill story; weakest as a share card, and it asks the most from the copy, since nothing but words is doing the entertaining.
