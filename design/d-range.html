Why this one could win

Palette
Enamel #F0E7D6, cast iron #211C19, copper #B5713A, ember #E4562A, flame #F6B23C, resting green #6E8F5E. The enamel warms and darkens as you cook: cool on the intake, scorched on the verdict, cooled again on the discharge — the background itself carries the story.
Type
A condensed poster face for the stamped nameplate and band names, an old-style serif for everything the app says out loud, and monospace kept down at label size — the enamel-era appliance voice, not a dashboard voice.
Kept from B
The knob is now bakelite with a copper pointer, and the pan sits on a cast-iron hob that gets its own inset panel on every screen. Both are load-bearing here rather than decorative: the knob is the score, the pan is the subject.
Language
Every forensic word is gone. No case number, no findings, no evidence, no severity — it is heat, pans, knives, orders on the pass, and resting.
Trade-off
Warmest and most on-brand of everything so far, and the knob still gives the demo its one big move. Costs some data density: the ticket holds three lines comfortably, not eight.
